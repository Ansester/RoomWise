Good to know.

A few practical answers for getting started and keeping control of your data.

How do chat, shopping pages, and the preview connect?

Describe a chair and press Send, then keep refining the request. Find matches searches from the accepted preferences. If a draft is still in the input, it is sent once first; with no draft, there is no additional model call. Swipe or step through RoomWise product pages and the selected chair changes in the phone preview automatically.

The in-room object is a schematic scaled to explicit assembled dimensions, not the retailer's exact 3D model. A product with unknown dimensions clears the size-based object until reliable measurements are supplied.

Which iPhone do I need?

RoomWise requires iOS 17 or later. RoomPlan scanning needs a LiDAR-equipped iPhone. Other supported phones can use a labelled sample or imported RoomWise JSON file. Camera AR needs supported hardware and camera access; Simulator provides a labelled 3D rehearsal. Hosted chat has no Apple Intelligence or device-specific model requirement.

Do I need paid services?

No paid model, RoomWise account, or hosted pairing server is needed. Optional live chat uses your personal Groq Free key and the hosted openai/gpt-oss-20b model. Discovery uses your personal Tavily Researcher free key. Keep those accounts on their free plans; provider terms, quotas, and external billing choices still apply.

Sample mode uses synthetic products and a labelled limited local conversation fallback without provider calls. The same fallback is available when Groq is unavailable; it is a deterministic parser, not an offline LLM. Without Tavily access, samples, retailer links built from accepted preferences, manual chair entry, and local previews remain available.

What are the chat and search limits?

Each conversational turn makes at most one Groq call, with at most twelve recent messages bounded to 1,500 characters each, plus accepted preferences. Send does not use Tavily. Find matches performs bounded catalog discovery separately; a nonempty draft is interpreted once before that search.

On iPhone, each discovery reserves three Tavily basic requests against a local allowance of 300 requests per UTC month. No extra extraction, advanced search, automatic retries, or paid-provider switch occurs. Desktop uses bounded searches without the persisted iPhone counter. Page changes and local pairing make no Groq or Tavily calls.

Check current Tavily plans and Groq limits. Your provider account can run out before the local counter if used elsewhere.

How do I browse on desktop and update my phone?

Use the same trusted Wi-Fi network. On desktop, choose Pair phone → Live phone preview → Start pairing. On iPhone, open Room actions → Pair with desktop, paste the complete private session link, and press Connect. Allow local-network access if asked. Desktop product-page changes then update the phone selection; phone selections can update desktop too.

Pairing uses a temporary plain-HTTP listener with a random bearer token. It shares products and selection, not room geometry, chat, camera data, or keys. Keep the link private and do not expose the port publicly. The phone uses its own accepted room. Pairing updates do not clear its conversation; you can keep chatting while paired. Pressing Find matches on the phone disconnects pairing before local discovery takes ownership. Stop pairing or disconnect when finished; a new session creates a new link.

Why is a paired phone not updating?

Check the complete current link, same local network, local-network permission, desktop firewall, and whether pairing is still running. Guest/university Wi-Fi may isolate devices; try a trusted network or personal hotspot. The phone polls about every 400 ms, disconnects in the background, and stops after three consecutive polling failures. Return to the foreground and reconnect when needed.

Does every retailer page change the object?

Swiping the known RoomWise product pages drives selection. On iPhone, Load retailer page explicitly opens an ephemeral embedded page. Following another page, a variant-related URL query/fragment change, or same-document navigation pauses the preview because that navigation does not establish validated dimensions for the newly viewed item. Return to a known RoomWise selection before relying on the object. A retailer can change variants without a detectable URL/navigation event; the preview remains tied to saved listing dimensions, so confirm the selected variant and its assembled measurements.

RoomWise does not monitor arbitrary browser tabs or scrape every product you visit. On desktop, Store opens the external browser without tracking its navigation. If an embedded retailer page is unavailable, open its original URL externally.

How do I preview a chair found elsewhere?

Add the chair with a name and optional supported retailer link or pasted listing details. Enter assembled width, depth, and height using reliable listing evidence or your own measurements. Package dimensions are not assembled size. User-entered facts are labelled, and missing dimensions remain unknown.

Why does a scan say “partial”?

The captured walls do not form a sufficiently complete room boundary. You can inspect a partial room, but RoomWise withholds boundary confirmation. Scan all walls, openings, and furniture where possible and review before accepting.

What does a fit check cover?

Checks compare a chair's rectangular floor footprint with captured walls and recognized furniture. They do not assess hidden obstacles, overhead clearance, movement, door swings, or delivery routes. Confirm the actual product and room before buying.

What is shared with the hosted providers?

After permission, Groq receives bounded recent chat and accepted shopping preferences. Tavily receives derived search requirements. Each provider receives only its own authentication key. Neither receives room coordinates, room counts, fit summaries, or camera images through this pipeline. Requests are associated with your personal provider accounts. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete chat, planning data, or keys?

New chat clears the active conversation and accepted preferences. The iPhone saves up to 40 recent chat messages/preferences locally; desktop keeps up to 24 messages in session memory. At most twelve recent messages are sent to Groq per turn.

Use Room actions → Help & Privacy → Delete saved rooms & searches to clear saved room, search, product, placement, conversation, preferences, and temporary export data. Clear provider keys separately in Connections. The local quota record remains separate. Exported files, backups, websites, and already-sent provider requests are managed through their respective controls. Stop/disconnect pairing separately when finished.

Why are there no matches or dimensions?

Discovery searches indexed retailer pages, not a complete Amazon, Noon, or Temu catalog. Listings may be unavailable, incomplete, or fail to satisfy preferences; account quotas, authentication, and network access can also fail. Sample products are filtered against hard constraints, so a refinement may have no matching sample. Inspect the status, revise the request, browse directly, or add reliable measurements manually.

Sample and live results are different.

Sample rooms and chairs are synthetic examples. Live prices, availability, and listing information can change. A dimensional preview helps you plan; confirm the current retailer listing and measure your space before ordering.
